# Bloc de style v2 (2026-11-21, après le tour 1)

Changements par rapport à la v1 ([style-v1.md](style-v1.md)), d'après le journal du tour 1 :

1. **Néon épais** (œuf de dragon) : nouvelle ligne sur les parties qui brillent.
2. **Échelle** (les 3 planches) : la mise en page dit le rapport de taille, plus seulement les dimensions.
3. **Créatures** (capybara) : on demande l'anatomie de l'animal dans la fiche de l'objet. Ce n'est pas dans le bloc, mais dans la description du sujet.

## Le bloc (en anglais)

```
STYLE — "Steal a Shop" chunky Roblox toy style, same family as our Exclusive Crate (reference image attached):
- Built like a premium Roblox crate: one big, simple, chunky main volume with LARGE FLAT FACES, wrapped by a thick SMOOTH FRAME on its edges and rims (like the gold frame of the crate). Small bevels on the frame only. Crisp, clean edges.
- The large flat faces of the main volume carry the classic Roblox STUDS texture (square stud tiles in relief). The frame, small details and faces are smooth, no studs.
- Exaggerated toy proportions: short, wide, sturdy. Nothing thin: no sticks, wires, strings, sharp spikes or tiny parts. Thin real-world parts are made thick and chunky.
- Glowing parts are BOLD THICK STRIPS or blocks, at least as wide as one stud tile, never thin lines or cracks drawn as strokes.
- Creatures are chibi: head about half of the total height, small body, short stubby legs, simple cute face (two black rounded-rectangle eyes with a small white highlight, small mouth), no fur or hair strands, body made of a few big rounded blocks. Keep the real animal's key shapes so it is recognizable.
- 3 to 5 flat, saturated colors. No gradients, no photo textures, no text, no logo.
- Rarity is shown by add-ons, not by more detail: Common and Uncommon = the object alone; Rare = frame in the rarity color; Epic = plus one glowing neon strip; Legendary and above = gold frame #FFC21A, neon strips and translucent crystals, like the Exclusive Crate.
- Rarity colors: Common #B0B7C3, Uncommon #2FE08F, Rare #2F9BFF, Epic #A855F7, Legendary #FFC21A, Mythic #FF3B5C.
- Rendered like a cartoon game asset: thick black outline around the silhouette, soft studio light, plain light background.
- Must read instantly at 40 studs away and as a 64 px icon.
```

## La mise en page de la planche (en anglais)

```
SHEET LAYOUT: front view, side view, back view and three-quarter view, same scale and same proportions in every view, on a plain light background. TRUE SCALE: on the left, a simple grey Roblox R15 character 5 studs tall; the item is drawn at its real size next to it, only about <H / 5> of the character's height (the item is <W> x <D> x <H> studs). The item must look small next to the character. A color palette strip with the hex colors at the bottom. No title banner, no rarity label, no text on the item itself.
```

Remplacer `<H / 5>` par une fraction simple : 1,8 stud donne « one third », 1,5 « less than one third », 2,0 « two fifths ».
