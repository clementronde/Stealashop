## Bubble tea

```
Model this "Bubble Tea" collectible item for a Roblox game, following the attached model sheet as the exact visual reference: same silhouette, proportions, colors and part layout. Follow the three-quarter view and the front view if anything disagrees. Same chunky toy style as our Exclusive Crate: big simple volumes, large flat faces, a thick smooth white frame on the rims with small bevels, crisp edges, nothing thin. Size: 1.0 wide x 1.0 deep x 1.8 high studs (1 unit = 1 stud), straw included. Separate parts, named in English PascalCase: Body (the pink cup), Frame (the thick white base rim and the white band under the lid), Frame2 (the domed lid), Accent (the dark tapioca pearls, chunky shapes on the lower part of the cup), Accent2 (the thick pink straw, at least 0.18 studs wide), Root (a flat 1.0 x 1.0 x 0.05 box under the base, hidden in game). Each part is one flat solid color: Body #F7A8C8, Frame and Frame2 #F5F7FA, Accent #3B2A2A, Accent2 #FF5FA2. Studs: do NOT model them. Keep the big flat faces of Body smooth; we apply a square-studs texture in Roblox. Low-poly: 600 triangles maximum. The cup is a 12-sided prism at most. Pivot at the bottom center, base flat on the ground (y = 0), front toward -Z. Do not include: any character, text or logo, a floor or pedestal, lights, particles or animation baked into the mesh. Export: one single .glb file, one object, flat solid-color materials.
```

## Capybara en peluche

```
Model this "Capybara Plush" collectible item for a Roblox game, following the attached model sheet as the exact visual reference: same silhouette, proportions, colors and part layout. Follow the side view for the head shape and the three-quarter view if anything disagrees. It must read as a capybara, not a bear: long boxy head with a big flat nose that sticks out forward, tiny ears high on the back of the head, barrel body, short stubby legs. Same chunky toy style as our Exclusive Crate: big simple blocky volumes with large flat faces, small bevels only, crisp edges, nothing thin. Size: 1.4 wide x 1.6 deep x 1.5 high studs (1 unit = 1 stud), orange included. Separate parts, named in English PascalCase: Body (back, sides and top of the head, the large flat faces), Frame (belly patch, nose and the four paws, smooth), Face (the closed eyes and the small mouth, slightly raised), Accent (the orange on the head, a chunky 12-sided ball with a leaf), Root (a flat 1.2 x 1.2 x 0.05 box under the base, hidden in game). Each part is one flat solid color: Body #B7834F, Frame #F2D9A6, Face #1E1E1E, Accent #FF9F1C (leaf #3FBF3A as part AccentLeaf). Studs: do NOT model them. Keep the big flat faces of Body smooth; we apply a square-studs texture in Roblox. Low-poly: 800 triangles maximum. Pivot at the bottom center, base flat on the ground (y = 0), the face looking toward -Z. Do not include: any character, text or logo, a floor or pedestal, lights, particles or animation baked into the mesh. Export: one single .glb file, one object, flat solid-color materials.
```

## Œuf de dragon

```
Model this "Dragon Egg" collectible item for a Roblox game, following the attached model sheet as the exact visual reference: same silhouette, proportions, colors and part layout. Follow the three-quarter view and the front view if anything disagrees. Same chunky toy style as our Exclusive Crate: big simple volumes, large flat faces, a thick smooth gold frame with small bevels, crisp edges, nothing thin. Size: 1.4 wide x 1.4 deep x 2.0 high studs (1 unit = 1 stud), nest and crystals included. Separate parts, named in English PascalCase: Body (the egg shell, faceted scale plates, dark purple), Body2 (the lighter purple scale plates), Frame (the thick gold band around the egg), Frame2 (the thick gold rim of the nest), Accent (the stone nest, chunky blocks), NeonCracks (the glowing cracks, bold thick zigzag strips raised on the shell, at least 0.1 studs wide), CrystalA, CrystalB, CrystalC (big chunky 6-sided crystals growing from the nest), Root (a flat 1.2 x 1.2 x 0.05 box under the base, hidden in game). Each part is one flat solid color: Body #7B2CFF, Body2 #B13CFF, Frame and Frame2 #FFC21A, Accent #8A8F99, NeonCracks #FF9F1C, crystals #5FF3FF. Every glowing part is its own piece in one flat color, so Roblox Studio can switch it to the Neon material. Studs: do NOT model them. Keep the big flat faces of Body and Body2 smooth; we apply a square-studs texture in Roblox. Low-poly: 1,200 triangles maximum. Crystals simple faceted shapes. Pivot at the bottom center, base flat on the ground (y = 0), front toward -Z. Do not include: any character, text or logo, a floor or pedestal, lights, particles or animation baked into the mesh. Export: one single .glb file, one object, flat solid-color materials. And the Roblox VFX pack of this entire dragon egg, with effects and VFX.
```
