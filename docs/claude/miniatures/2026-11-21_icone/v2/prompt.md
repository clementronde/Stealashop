## Prompt 1

> These are from a real Roblox game: the wide image is a raw in-game screenshot, the square image is my rough crop of
> it showing the framing I want. Retouch them into the game's icon (square 1:1). It must still show exactly this
> in-game moment, with the same scene, camera angle and elements: do not add any object, character or place that is
> not in the screenshot. The scene, seen from inside a jewelry store: a blocky Roblox R15 avatar (orange hoodie, dark
> navy pants, beige skin) standing in the street smashes the store's display window with a big sledgehammer (violet
> handle, dark head with a glowing pink stripe); the glass shatters at the impact point into big transparent shards
> and many small light-blue glass cubes bursting toward the viewer; in the foreground, inside the store, a huge golden
> crown (red velvet cap, white pearls, gold cross on top) glowing purple, its rarity color. Make it read instantly on
> a tiny 150 px tile: 1) tighter framing, with the avatar and the window much bigger; 2) the sledgehammer clearly
> recognizable as a sledgehammer (seen from the side, head hitting the glass), at the exact moment of impact, with a
> bright white impact flash and radial cracks around it; 3) a simple Roblox-style face with an excited open-mouth grin
> and raised eyebrows; 4) a thick dark outline (#1B1B24) around the avatar, the sledgehammer and the crown; 5) the
> crown's gold (#FFC21A) as the brightest point of the image, with its purple glow (#A855F7); 6) a simple background:
> the street blurred into a sky blue (#8FD0FF) that darkens toward the corners; 7) colors a bit more saturated than
> the screenshot, but it must still look like Roblox in-game 3D (smooth blocky plastic), not realistic, not anime, not
> a painting. Keep everything important inside the central circle, since the corners will be rounded. No text,
> letters, numbers, logo, UI or watermark.

Résultat : **version 1**, cadrage serré, très lisible à 150 px. Retenue comme base.
