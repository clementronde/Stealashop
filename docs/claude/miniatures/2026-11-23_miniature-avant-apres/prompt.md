## Prompt 1

> Make a new Roblox game thumbnail, wide 16:9, in exactly the same blocky Roblox 3D style as the attached image (same
> thick dark outlines, same bright saturated colors) and with the same masked bacon hair character, but a completely
> new scene: a BEFORE / AFTER comparison of the player's own shop, split down the middle by a thin white diagonal
> line. LEFT HALF, before (level 1): a tiny, poor, run-down wooden market stall with a torn grey cloth awning and a
> crooked plank counter; on the counter, a single dented soda can, the only item for sale. Next to it stands the
> masked bacon hair (black balaclava with an open mouth hole, brown bacon hair, black top with a blue tie, black
> pants, white sneakers, light skin hands), small, sad and disappointed: drooping shoulders, sad eyebrows, a frown and
> a tear. Slightly duller, greyer colors. RIGHT HALF, after (level 999): the same masked bacon hair, much bigger, with
> a huge proud grin, arms spread wide, standing in front of a huge luxurious golden shop with golden pillars, big
> glass windows and a red and gold awning, overflowing with glowing treasures on display: a giant golden crown with a
> purple glow, a diamond treasure chest, a golden trophy and a gaming PC, each with its own colored glow; gold coins
> and green dollar bills fly around him. Bright, saturated, golden sparkles. Simple background with high color
> contrast on both sides: clear blue sky with no clouds and bright green grass. Layout for a phone screen: both
> characters big and readable, free sky above the soda can on the left and above the treasures on the right for money
> labels, free space at the top center for an arrow, nothing important in the bottom 20%. No text at all: no letters,
> numbers, signs, logo, UI or watermark.

Résultat : [apercu_chatgpt_1.png](apercu_chatgpt_1.png). Le concept se lit bien, mais ChatGPT a dessiné sa propre
flèche jaune en haut, les deux persos ont la même taille et le ciel manque au-dessus de la boutique dorée.
